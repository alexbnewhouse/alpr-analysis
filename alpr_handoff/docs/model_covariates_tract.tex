\begin{minipage}{\textwidth}
\footnotesize
\textit{Notes.} All ACS quantities are 2023 5-year estimates. Each share is
computed over its own ACS universe rather than over total population.

\textbf{Log population is deliberately excluded.} It is the exact sum of
\texttt{log\_density} and \texttt{log\_area}, so entering all three would be
perfectly collinear; the two retained terms span the same space.

\textbf{Density enters quadratically} because the share of tracts with no
camera is non-monotone in density --- 63.2\% in the sparsest quintile, 45.0\%
in the middle, 63.1\% in the densest. A linear term would average two opposite
tails into a single misleading slope.

\textbf{Race shares are compositional} and sum to approximately one, so
\texttt{pct\_white} is omitted as the reference category; entering all shares
is singular.

\textbf{Owner-occupied share is the exact complement} of renter share and is
never entered jointly.

Variables excluded on collinearity grounds, each correlating above $0.69$ with
median household income and describing the same latent factor: per-capita
income, median home value, median gross rent, and share with a bachelor's
degree or higher. Variables excluded as bad controls, being consequences of
urban form that would absorb the density coefficient: share of households with
no vehicle, share driving alone, and share using public transit.
