\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\footnotesize
\textit{Notes.} ``Source'' denotes a variable taken directly from the provider;
``Constructed'' denotes one we derive. All ACS estimates are 2023 5-year.
Each share is computed over its own ACS universe --- education over adults
25 and older, poverty over the poverty universe, commute shares over workers
--- rather than over total population. Census jam values ($-666666666$ and
similar) are recoded to missing. Race shares are compositional and sum to
approximately one, so \texttt{pct\_white} is omitted as the reference category
in estimation. \texttt{pct\_owner\_occupied} and \texttt{pct\_renter\_occupied}
are exact complements and are never entered jointly. Crime is measured at the
jurisdiction that reports it and broadcast to smaller units; it does not vary
within a county. The raw ACS numerators and denominators behind each derived
share (\texttt{white\_alone}, \texttt{race\_total}, \texttt{poverty\_count},
\texttt{edu\_total}, and so on) are retained in the data files so that any
share can be re-based, but are omitted from this table for brevity.
